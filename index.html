2. Purpose of the Application

The application is designed to help manage the creation and preparation of promotional Pinterest Pins for products sold through the SoftQuoteArt Etsy shop.

3. Information Processed

Depending on the features enabled and authorized, the application may process:

  • Pinterest account identifiers and authorization tokens;
  • Pinterest board identifiers and related information;
  • Pin titles, descriptions, images and destination links;
  • Product information and promotional content;
  • Technical logs and error information required for troubleshooting.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the automation workflow, prepare promotional content, connect to authorized services, and support Pinterest publishing functionality when enabled.

Personal information is not sold to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with the following services:

  • Pinterest API, for authorized Pinterest functionality;
  • OpenAI API, for generating promotional text and related content;
  • Etsy, as the destination platform for promoted products.

These services process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Information is transmitted when required by the functionality being used.
